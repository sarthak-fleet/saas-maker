# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors evaluating Sarthak Agrawal's public products, existing users looking
for the canonical product or source link, and search or AI agents discovering
the maintained portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Publish a precise, durable map of maintained products from Fleet's canonical
metadata. The directory explains what each product does, where it lives, what
changed, and what is planned without exposing private operations.

## Positioning

One builder's maintained products, presented with their real domains,
repositories, changelogs, and roadmaps.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: open a product's canonical site.
- Secondary CTA: inspect its changelog, roadmap, or source.
- Tertiary CTA: propose a serious, paid external product build by email. This
  remains selective and subordinate to the maintained-product portfolio.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a clear, living directory of
  curious products forged by one builder.
- Belief ladder: the products are real; their ownership and status are clear;
  their progress is inspectable; the directory stays current because Fleet
  generates it.
- Proof on hand: canonical domains, product-owned public changelogs, public
  repositories, and repository-native GitHub Issues roadmaps.

## Brand Personality

Curious, exact, and quietly ambitious. The site should feel considered and
human rather than corporate, technical, or dashboard-like.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble an admin console, task tracker, observability dashboard, generic
SaaS card grid, or AI-generated startup template. Do not expose Fleet controls,
local machine state, schedules, private repositories, or operational telemetry.

## Design Principles

- Lead with products, not infrastructure.
- Show evidence through canonical links rather than invented metrics.
- Keep the spotlight concise while preserving the complete public catalog.
- Let one visual identity carry the directory without merging product brands.
- Make the same public facts legible to people, search engines, and AI agents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and interaction requirements, support keyboard and touch
navigation, preserve browser zoom, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
