# Composition patterns and originality

Choose one primary family. Combining several usually produces a slide rather
than an editorial illustration.

## Composition families

- **Process:** input, transformation, output. Xiaohei or a strange machine
  performs the transformation while one orange path shows direction.
- **System fragment:** show only 3-5 essential parts of a source, filter,
  repository, renderer, or agent system. Xiaohei owns one critical operation.
- **Before and after:** contrast disorder/order, manual/automatic,
  scattered/collected, or anxious/stable, with one transition between them.
- **Character states:** use 2-4 small moments for a pain point, overload,
  blockage, or change in working state.
- **Conceptual metaphor:** turn the idea into one memorable low-tech machine or
  oversized object with a few inputs and one output.
- **Layered method:** stack imperfect boxes or physical layers while Xiaohei
  builds, carries, or braces them. Avoid a formal pyramid.
- **Route:** use one winding path with a few meaningful stops. Xiaohei walks,
  guides, opens, or reconnects it.
- **Mini comic:** use 2-4 scenes for an actual sequence or reversal, with one
  action per panel.

## Invent a fresh metaphor

1. Translate the abstract idea into a physical verb: snag, leak, weigh, sort,
   settle, ferment, unlock, fold, unpack, or recirculate.
2. Translate the system into one or two low-tech objects: drawer, broken
   machine, box, pipe, mailbox, scale, well, ladder, gate, dial, punch, press,
   clothesline, or odd workstation.
3. Give Xiaohei the verb: the character pulls, catches, stuffs, mends, marks,
   balances, opens, or recycles the object.

Do not reuse a known illustration merely because the article has a similar
topic. Preserve the idea but change both the main object and Xiaohei's action.
Examples calibrate density and restraint; they are never layout templates.
