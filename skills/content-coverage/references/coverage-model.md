# Coverage model

## Evidence

Use four sources:

1. product truth: shipped behavior, docs, pricing, privacy, supported platforms;
2. owned inventory: sitemap, routes, content collections, canonical URLs,
   internal links, freshness, and indexability;
3. current search and competitor evidence for customer intents; and
4. first-party performance evidence when deliberately connected.

Timestamp external evidence. Mark unavailable inputs explicitly.

## Archetypes

Evaluate only archetypes supported by intent and differentiation:

- category and product explanation;
- problem and solution;
- feature and workflow;
- use case and audience;
- integration;
- how-to and tutorial;
- comparison and alternatives;
- proof, case study, benchmark, or methodology;
- template, checklist, glossary, and reference.

No fixed page count is healthy for every product.

## Verdicts

For each intent/archetype choose:

- `keep`: strong, current, differentiated, internally linked;
- `update`: right URL but stale, shallow, or weakly evidenced;
- `merge`: overlaps another page and risks cannibalization;
- `create`: meaningful intent is absent and can be answered truthfully;
- `redirect`: retired or consolidated URL has link/user value;
- `prune`: thin or misleading page has no durable value;
- `blocked`: needed claims or product capability cannot be verified;
- `research`: evidence is insufficient to decide.

Prioritize with customer relevance, evidence strength, differentiation,
business proximity, current ranking opportunity, and production cost. Domain
rating, dofollow status, competitor volume, and raw word count are not quality
signals by themselves.

## Draft and claim ledger

Every draft includes:

- canonical slug, title, meta description, primary intent and audience;
- concise answer first, then complete useful content;
- source-backed product statements and dated external claims;
- honest limitations and comparison basis;
- internal links to the product, adjacent education, proof, and conversion;
- appropriate structured data only when the visible page supports it;
- one claim-ledger row per material claim: claim, source, checked time, owner,
  and status.

Block unsupported superlatives, unverifiable competitor claims, fabricated
reviews, and performance numbers without evidence.
