## Design Specificity Verdict

The page is highly authored and product-specific. The workshop manifesto cannot be swapped onto a generic AI agency: the owner voice, accountable inventory, direct public evidence, and deliberate inclusion of non-AI work make the studio’s position credible. The cobalt AI bay is the visual and narrative peak, while representative products now prove the thesis immediately after it.

The deterministic CLI scan returned zero findings. Browser overlay inspection initially reported eight advisory patterns: two seeded-pane contrast failures and unconstrained reading measures were verified and fixed; oversized display type and tight headline leading were reviewed as intentional workshop-language false positives. Final three-width browser evidence showed zero horizontal overflow and zero console/page errors.

## Overall Impression

This is a confident founder-led studio statement, not an inflated company page. The biggest gain came from moving products ahead of the conceptual explanation and exposing their canonical destinations and sources directly.

## What’s Working

- The first viewport gives AI a precise role without making “AI studio” the identity.
- Four representative products demonstrate range while remaining catalog-backed and verifiable.
- The close is honest about scope and now offers both the complete register and a selective serious-build path.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Founder presence remains visually abstract.** The founder is named and linked, but the page relies entirely on typography. If a real, suitable studio or maker image becomes available, one physical artifact could strengthen the human presence without changing the visual language. Suggested command: `$impeccable delight`.
- **[P3] Studio has no active primary-navigation state.** The homepage gives it a clear entry point, but a visitor who lands directly on `/studio` cannot see “Studio” as a current nav location. Avoid adding a sixth cramped mobile item unless the navigation is restructured. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P3] Some thesis language is intentionally abstract.** “AI as material” is memorable, but first-time visitors may need the representative work to decode it. The current early proof placement makes this acceptable. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.
