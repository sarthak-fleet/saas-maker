# QA checklist

## Must pass

- The image is horizontal 16:9 on a clean white background.
- Xiaohei is present and performs the action that explains the idea.
- The metaphor was invented for the current article.
- The result is strange and memorable but still clear within a second.
- The main subject occupies no more than roughly 60% of the canvas.
- The image explains one relationship, action, or state.
- English labels are few, short, correctly spelled, and legible.
- Orange marks only the main path; red marks only the key problem or result;
  blue marks only secondary context.

## Failure signals

Regenerate or edit if:

- the corner contains a generic title such as "Workflow", "Architecture",
  "Common mistakes", or "Roadmap";
- Xiaohei looks like a cute mascot, reaction sticker, or children's character;
- the image resembles a presentation, course page, flowchart, or architecture
  diagram;
- there are too many nodes, arrows, objects, or explanatory sentences;
- the background contains texture, beige tone, noise, gradients, or shadows;
- the scene includes a realistic UI or glossy technology interface;
- a label is misspelled, illegible, or differs from the requested phrase;
- the metaphor is stiff, generic, or too close to a previous composition.

## Corrections

- **Too ordinary:** make Xiaohei cause the central transformation through one
  strange but coherent physical action.
- **Too complex:** remove nodes and keep one action with 3-5 labels.
- **Too cute:** reinforce deadpan, blank, serious, irregular, and not mascot.
- **Too diagrammatic:** remove the title, grid, borders, and surplus arrows;
  replace them with a physical scene.
- **Too derivative:** preserve the meaning but change the main object and the
  character's verb.
- **Bad text:** edit a single error; regenerate with fewer labels when several
  labels fail.

A strong result feels slightly strange first, then makes the relationship
obvious. If it looks like a tutorial slide at first glance, it fails.
