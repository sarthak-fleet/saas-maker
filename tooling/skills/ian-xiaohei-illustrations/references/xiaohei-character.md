# Xiaohei character

Xiaohei is Ian's recurring visual character: a deadpan worker inside an absurd
system, not a mascot or sticker.

## Appearance

- A small solid-black creature with white dot eyes and thin legs; thin arms are
  optional.
- The body may resemble a black bean, short cylinder, uneven box, funnel,
  shadow, opening, or dark part of a machine.
- The silhouette is slightly irregular and hand-drawn.
- The expression is blank, calm, serious, and a little bewildered.

## Behavior

Xiaohei takes the action that makes the metaphor legible: hauling, pulling,
feeding, catching, sorting, pressing, weighing, stitching, cutting, turning,
guarding, pushing, repairing, recording, or recycling. The action must explain
the article's idea rather than add arbitrary weirdness.

Xiaohei can be earnest, awkward, and dryly funny, but never foolish or cute.
Avoid costumes, expressive reaction faces, sparkling eyes, glossy rendering,
or a polished corporate mascot silhouette.

## Test

Mentally remove Xiaohei. If the complete metaphor still works unchanged,
Xiaohei is decorative. Rewrite the scene so the character causes, carries,
repairs, blocks, or transforms the central relationship.
