# Channel quality

## Protected channels

Hacker News, LinkedIn, and X are reputation-bearing channels. Each asset must
have a clear audience, original angle, accurate claim ledger, useful substance,
native format, specific CTA, and appropriate visual. Do not spray one generic
announcement across them or silently execute copy approved for another lane.

## Broad distribution

Broad distribution is acceptable only when:

- the product fits the destination's active audience and policy;
- the listing/post is accurate and uses destination-specific required fields;
- the same community is not flooded with duplicates;
- the account represents the real owner/product;
- there is no fabricated testimonial, vote, review, or engagement;
- cost and moderation state are visible in preview.

Low effort means concise adaptation, not irrelevant or deceptive spam.

For articles, prefer a complete canonical-attributed copy where supported,
then an editorial pitch or destination-native discovery post. Never truncate a
strong article merely to multiply URLs.

For backlink work, attempted forms and unverified profile claims are not
success. Record visible live links, deduplicate by referring domain, and retain
follow state, indexability, likely permanence, and topical fit for later
quality decisions.

## Registry reconciliation

`ops/config/directory-submissions/directories.json` and
`ops/config/directory-submissions/research-probe.json` are lead lists. Load
them through the channel-inventory helper, then reverify every retained
destination live. Do not use stored dofollow/DR, cost, form, or authentication
claims as current authority. Convert stale automation labels to current
`connector`, `postiz`, `browser`, `manual`, or `blocked` modes with a timestamp
and reason.

Exclude legacy `directory-submit` spray code. It uses hidden browser behavior,
force submission, and ambiguous success signals that violate this skill.
